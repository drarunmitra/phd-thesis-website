2.1 Introduction

While developing data science solutions for public health, it is important to understand the context in which the study has been conducted. This research aims to develop data science solutions through participatory engagement to enhance maternal and child health (MCH) service delivery at the local level for mid-level program managers. The study focuses on the Integrated Tribal Development Agency (ITDA), Rampachodavaram, a tribal area in Andhra Pradesh, India.
